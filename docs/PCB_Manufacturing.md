# PCB Manufacturing

- Board: 90 × 40 mm
- Layers: 2
- Copper: 1 oz

## Fabrication Package
Include:
- Gerber (.gbr)
- Drill (.drl)
- .gbrjob

## Recommended PCB Houses
- JLCPCB
- PCBWay
- Seeed Fusion
- OSH Park

## Checklist
- DRC passed
- Gerbers verified
- Drill files generated
